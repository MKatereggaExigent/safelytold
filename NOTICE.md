# Important safety and legal notice

This is a foundational engineering scaffold, not production software or legal, medical,
employment-relations, investigation, or security advice. Do not accept real allegations or
identity data until legal review, a DPIA, threat modelling, penetration testing, processor
assurance, incident exercises, and handler training are complete.

Never promise absolute anonymity. Never place identities, narratives, evidence, recovery
secrets, case IDs, or low-entropy direct hashes on a blockchain.
