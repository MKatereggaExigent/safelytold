# HELP ME Independent Advisory Group – Charter

## 1. Mandate

Provide independent guidance and oversight across ethics, labour law, privacy, investigations, psychology, union/employee voice and security, ensuring the HELP ME platform maintains trust commitments and legal obligations.

## 2. Scope of Work

| Area | Responsibilities |
| --- | --- |
| Trust & Governance | Review updates to the Trust Charter, tenant prohibited-use clauses, fairness policy and public trust-centre content. |
| Product Safety | Evaluate new features (especially AI, analytics, monitoring) for risks to reporters, witnesses and accused parties; require mitigations before release. |
| Legal & Privacy | Advise on POPIA/GDPR/local law changes, retention policies, cross-border transfers, DPIAs, DSAR handling. |
| Investigations & Psychology | Validate process design (triage, interviews, retaliation checks) against trauma-informed and fair-process standards. |
| Employee Voice | Ensure deployment plans include consultation with works councils/unions and provide transparent escalation paths. |
| Security & Incident Response | Review threat models, break-glass requests, breach reports and remediation roadmaps. |

## 3. Composition

- Minimum seven members covering: employment/labour law (2), privacy/data protection (1), investigations/ombuds (1), workplace psychology (1), union/employee advocate (1), security/zero trust (1).
- Members serve staggered 12‑month terms; may be renewed once.
- Conflicts of interest must be declared; members recuse when reviewing tenants/partners they advise elsewhere.

## 4. Meetings & Cadence

- Regular session monthly (virtual) with agenda circulated 5 days in advance.
- Extraordinary sessions may be convened within 72 hours for incidents or urgent approvals.
- Quorum: ≥50% of seated members including at least one legal/privacy specialist and one worker representative.

## 5. Deliverables

1. Meeting minutes recorded in `/docs/trust/advisory/minutes-YYYYMM.md` (public version minus sensitive tenant data).
2. Recommendation tracker noting decisions, required mitigations, deadlines and accountable owners.
3. Annual report summarising privacy/security posture, unresolved risks and tenant trends (aggregated).

## 6. Powers

- **Veto authority** over features or contractual changes that violate the Trust Charter.
- **Escalation authority** to demand independent investigation or notify regulators if the platform refuses to remediate severe issues.
- **Access rights** to relevant artefacts (threat models, DPIAs, audit logs, AI evaluations) under confidentiality agreements.

## 7. Membership Process

1. Candidate nomination (by founder, existing members, or advisory alumni).
2. Evaluation against diversity and expertise matrix.
3. Approval via simple majority vote.
4. Formal onboarding: NDA, code of conduct, conflict-of-interest declaration, orientation pack.

## 8. Code of Conduct

- Maintain confidentiality of case-level data unless legally required disclosure is approved by majority vote.
- Disclose conflicts proactively; abstain from votes where conflict exists.
- Prioritise reporter safety and fairness over commercial considerations.
- Avoid accepting gifts/compensation from tenants beyond disclosed consulting arrangements.

## 9. Administrative Support

- Platform provides secretariat duties (agenda prep, note taking, document management).
- Meeting materials stored in version-controlled repository with access limited to advisory members and designated staff.

## 10. Review

Charter reviewed annually; amendments require two-thirds advisory approval plus founder concurrence.
