# Design Partner Ideal Customer Profile

## Target Attributes
- Size: 1,000–10,000 employees with distributed sites.
- Industry: Financial services, retail, energy, public sector with high compliance exposure.
- Pain points: Legacy hotline, poor anti-retaliation controls, multi-language workforce, union engagement.
- Champions: Chief Ethics/Compliance Officer, CHRO, Integrity Committee chair.
- Requirements: Sovereign hosting (ZA or regionally), POPIA compliance, independent escalation, willingness to co-design & publish lessons (anonymised).

## Disqualifiers
- Refusal to grant independent escalation.
- Desire for covert monitoring or loyalty scoring.
- No executive sponsorship / unwilling to expose governance gaps.

## Value Proposition Draft
- Conflict-safe routing, sealed evidence, retaliation protection, governance transparency.
