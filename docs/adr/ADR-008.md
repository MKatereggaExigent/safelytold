# ADR-008: AI Cannot Make Adverse Employment Decisions

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Context:** §14.1–14.3
- **Decision:** AI/LLM features are limited to clerical assistance (drafting, translation, chronology, policy retrieval with citations). They cannot render judgments about credibility, guilt, performance, or discipline. Human approval with documented rationale is mandatory for every AI output shown to investigators or reporters.
- **Consequences:**
  - Reduces legal/ethical exposure, aligns with EU AI Act high-risk categories.
  - Requires policy enforcement in AI gateway + UI disclaimers.
- **Next Steps:** Bake guardrails into AI service design, track evaluations per use case.
