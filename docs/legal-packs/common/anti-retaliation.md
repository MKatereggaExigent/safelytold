# Anti-Retaliation & Independent Escalation Rider

Attach this rider to every design-partner or tenant agreement. It gives contractual force to the Trust Charter (see `docs/trust/charter.md`) and satisfies proposal requirements (§8.5, Appendix F).

## 1. Non-Retaliation Covenant

1. Tenant shall not discharge, demote, harass, discriminate or otherwise retaliate against any employee, contractor, witness, supporter or case handler for:
   - Submitting a report or journal entry in good faith.
   - Participating in an investigation or providing evidence.
   - Raising concerns about process fairness, privacy or safety.
   - Contacting an external ombuds, regulator or legal authority where legally permitted.
2. Retaliation allegations trigger immediate review by the tenant’s integrity committee and the platform’s trust team.
3. Verified retaliation constitutes material breach and may result in suspension or termination of the tenant’s licence.

## 2. Independent Escalation

1. Tenant designates an external ombuds / law firm / integrity committee empowered to receive cases where:
   - Reported parties include senior leadership or compliance staff.
   - Reporters express fear of internal handling.
   - The platform’s conflict engine flags unsafe routing.
2. HELP ME will route such cases automatically; tenant must honour escalations without interference.
3. External reviewers receive scoped access, budget and authority to recommend remediation.

## 3. Safe Contact & Check‑ins

1. Tenant commits to offering reporters a safe contact channel (anonymous mailbox, external hotline or ombuds) for retaliation concerns.
2. For high-risk cases, tenant will perform scheduled check-ins (cadence defined in protection plan) and record outcomes in the platform.

## 4. Transparency & Audit

1. Tenant provides quarterly summary of retaliation allegations/outcomes to the platform (aggregated, anonymised).
2. Records must be retained for ≥5 years or statutory minimum, whichever is longer.

## 5. Remedies

1. Where retaliation is substantiated, tenant must enact corrective action (reinstate, compensate, discipline perpetrators, update policy) and log evidence within 30 days.
2. Platform may require third-party monitor or temporary suspension for repeated violations.

## 6. Notice to Employees

Tenant will publish plain-language guidance (in all relevant languages) describing:

- HELP ME’s confidentiality guarantees and limitations.
- How independent escalation works.
- Assurance that retaliation is prohibited and how to report concerns.
- Available support resources (EAP, legal aid, counsellors).

## 7. Enforcement

Failure to meet these commitments triggers the remedies outlined in the Master Subscription Agreement and may be disclosed to the advisory board, regulators or prospective employees.
