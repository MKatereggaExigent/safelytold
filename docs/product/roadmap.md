# Delivery roadmap

## Phase 0 — discovery and assurance

Employee/whistleblower research, independent advisory group, legal map, brand testing, prototype, DPIA, LINDDUN/STRIDE review and three committed design partners.

## Phase 1 — protected-reporting MVP

Private journal, reporting modes, mailbox, evidence vault, conflict routing, case/investigation process, anti-retaliation follow-up, append-only audit, Temporal workflow, basic thresholded reporting and handler training.

## Phase 2 — enterprise readiness

OIDC/SAML/SCIM, HRIS graph, dedicated tenancy, multilingual and assisted/voice channels, external ombuds, legal packs, data residency, CMK, 24×7 enterprise security monitoring, SOC 2/ISO 27001 preparation and independent penetration testing.

## Phase 3 — bounded intelligence and scale

Governed AI gateway, translation, anonymity hints, redaction, chronology, policy retrieval, summaries, thresholded patterns, partner marketplace, sovereign deployment and documented model/AI impact evaluations.
