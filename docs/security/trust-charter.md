# Trust charter

1. Protect reporters without presuming allegations are true or false.
2. Protect respondents through notice, impartiality, evidence-based findings and appeal.
3. Never sell case data or use it for advertising.
4. Never use AI to score guilt, credibility, reputation, promotion, dismissal or mental health.
5. Never place personal or case data on a blockchain.
6. Separate identity from case content and make exceptional identity access visible and dual-controlled.
7. Prevent implicated or conflicted people from controlling a case.
8. Detect and respond to retaliation concerns throughout and after a case.
9. Publish understandable privacy, security, AI and process metrics.
10. Give customers control without giving ordinary tenant administrators unrestricted reporter identity access.
