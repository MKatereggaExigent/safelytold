# Client shared-responsibility model

| Control area | SafelyTold | Client/tenant | Shared |
|---|---|---|---|
| Platform security | Secure SDLC, service isolation, patches, monitoring, evidence integrity | Approved networks/devices where mandated | Incident coordination and testing |
| Identity | OIDC integration, MFA support, role/purpose enforcement | Authoritative joiner/mover/leaver data, role approval, IdP security | SSO/SCIM configuration and access reviews |
| Case confidentiality | Technical need-to-know controls and audit | Safe assignments, conflicts, fair process, endpoint confidentiality | Break-glass and identity-access approval |
| Privacy | Processor/operator controls, subprocessor transparency, DSAR tooling | Controller/responsible-party lawful basis, notices, retention/legal decisions | DPIA, incidents, requests and transfers |
| Reporting channels | Secure web and provider integration | Publish verified routes and organisational commitments | Email/hotline provider selection and testing |
| Data quality | Correction and source-linked records | Timely, fair handling and authorised updates | DSAR/correction resolution |
| Availability | Platform SLO/design, backups and recovery | Client continuity contacts and alternative channels | Exercises, incident communications and SLA review |
| Staff training | Materials, workflow and product training | Attendance, competence, refresher and consequences | Role-specific simulations and QA |
| Devices/network | Secure browser/session design | Managed staff endpoints, malware protection, physical security | Session/device policy proportional to privacy |
| Legal compliance | Configurable jurisdiction packs and controls | Local counsel, policy adoption, worker consultation | Configuration review and evidence |

Clients must not attempt to identify anonymous reporters, export content into uncontrolled tools, grant
seniority-based access, use allegation counts as guilt/performance scores, or permit conflicted handlers.
SafelyTold may suspend unsafe integration/access patterns under contract and preserve audit evidence.
