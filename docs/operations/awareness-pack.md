# Reporter awareness pack

This is a tenant-configurable template. Each subscribing organisation publishes approved content on its
posters, intranet, induction slides, wallet cards and accessible digital formats in its selected languages.

**Speak up safely.** Report workplace misconduct online or through the toll-free hotline, 24 hours a day.
You may report anonymously, confidentially or with your name. You receive a private case reference and
recovery secret. Keep both safe; SafelyTold cannot recover a lost anonymous secret.

Use the secure mailbox for questions and updates. Avoid a work device or account if that feels unsafe.
Share facts you believe are true; you do not need to prove the case. Retaliation for a good-faith report
is prohibited—report any concern through the mailbox or hotline immediately.

This is not an emergency line. For immediate danger contact local emergency services first. The privacy
notice explains access, retention and rights.

Before publication insert tenant-specific organisation name, verified web address, toll-free number,
languages, emergency numbers, response SLA, privacy notice and QR code. The tenant's authorised
communications, legal/privacy and employee-representative stakeholders approve translations; SafelyTold
and the tenant test links and routing monthly under the shared-responsibility model.
