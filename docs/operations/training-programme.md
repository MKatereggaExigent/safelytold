# Customer training programme

Training is role-based and completed before access is activated.

- All users (30 min): reporting modes, confidentiality limits, safe communication and anti-retaliation.
- Hotline operators (4 h plus observed simulation): neutral listening, minimisation, categorisation,
  reference delivery, interpreters, high-risk escalation and outage procedure.
- Handlers/investigators (1 day): fair process, conflicts, evidence, privilege, protection, appeals/audit.
- Managers/oversight (2 h): thresholded reports, prohibited re-identification and SLA governance.
- Administrators/on-call (4 h plus drill): least privilege, MFA, break glass, backups and incidents.

Pass mark is 80%; critical privacy/safety questions require 100%. Failed learners retrain before access.
Keep attendance, assessment, trainer, content version and expiry in the LMS. Refresh annually, after
material changes and relevant QA findings. Run twice-yearly incident simulations.
