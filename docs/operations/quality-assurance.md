# Operational quality assurance

The QA lead is independent of the sampled case handler. QA assesses process quality, never whether a
reporter is "credible".

Before launch test every reporting mode and taxonomy route; reference recovery; mailbox and evidence;
malware handling; conflicts; escalations; protection; reports; retention/legal hold; DSAR; audit
verification; role isolation; restore/failover; rate limits; accessibility; languages and hotline calls.

Monthly review all critical cases plus a risk-weighted 10% sample of other cases (minimum 10 where volume
allows). Score timeliness, classification, conflicts, least privilege, communication, evidence handling,
decision rationale, appeal rights, retaliation follow-up and closure. Never export narratives to QA files.

Identity exposure, cross-tenant access, lost evidence, missed life-safety escalation and broken audit
integrity stop release or trigger incident response. Other findings get owner, deadline and verification.
Production approval requires signed functional, security, privacy, continuity and operations gates.
