# Management reporting standard

Issue the monthly report by the fifth business day; provide ad-hoc reports only to authorised requesters.
Reports contain counts and trends, never narratives or direct identifiers. Suppress small cohorts using
the configured privacy threshold.

Include reports by channel/category/site; backlog by status/age; acknowledgement/resolution SLA;
escalations; conflict recusals; retaliation/protection; reopened/appealed cases; hotline answer,
abandonment and failover; mailbox response; privacy incidents; QA defects and overdue actions. Show
denominator, period, comparison and suppression. Separate allegations from substantiated findings.

Record purpose, requester, filters, generation time, owner and approval. Ad-hoc exports expire, are
encrypted and logged. Reports must not be used to infer identity or rank complainants.
