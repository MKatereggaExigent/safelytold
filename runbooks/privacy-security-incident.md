# Privacy and security incident

1. Preserve safety: do not expose reporters while investigating the breach.
2. Contain credentials, tokens, integrations and affected workloads.
3. Open a restricted incident record and emit `privacy.security_incident.v1` without case content.
4. Preserve forensic logs under legal hold; do not copy restricted case content into general incident tools.
5. Determine people, tenants, jurisdictions, data classes and timing affected.
6. Involve DPO/privacy, security, labour/legal and customer incident contacts.
7. Assess notification duties using reviewed jurisdiction packs and counsel.
8. Rotate keys/credentials and validate chain, evidence and database integrity.
9. Restore from verified backups and monitor retaliation or reporter-safety consequences.
10. Complete root cause, corrective actions and independent review.
