# Defensible evidence export

1. Verify authority, purpose, jurisdiction and conflict status.
2. Freeze the requested scope and apply legal hold.
3. Export sealed originals plus authorised derivatives; never silently transform originals.
4. Generate a canonical manifest with object hash, size, copy type, timestamps and custody events.
5. Build a disclosure-specific Merkle root and optionally anchor it.
6. Encrypt the package to the approved recipient; transfer keys separately.
7. Require recipient acknowledgement and record disclosure audit entries.
8. Independently verify every hash after transfer.
9. Expire temporary export storage according to policy.
