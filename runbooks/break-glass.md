# Break-glass access

1. Declare an incident or documented urgent legal/safety need; ordinary convenience is not sufficient.
2. Request a time-limited purpose-bound grant naming case, fields and actions.
3. Obtain two independent approvals; requester cannot approve.
4. Notify privacy/security duty officer and, when lawful, the tenant ombuds.
5. Issue a short-lived credential through PAM; prohibit bulk export.
6. Record every read/write in the separate audit store.
7. Revoke automatically at expiry or earlier.
8. Review within 24 hours; notify affected people when lawful and safe.
9. Treat unexplained access as a security/privacy incident.
