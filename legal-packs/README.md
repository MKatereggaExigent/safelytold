# Jurisdiction packs

These are configuration scaffolds, not legal advice. Every deployment requires review by qualified employment, whistleblowing, privacy, evidence and records-management counsel in the relevant jurisdictions.

A pack configures reporting channels, acknowledgement targets, retention review triggers, identity-disclosure approvals, protected-disclosure notices, works-council/union considerations, language resources and regulator escalation placeholders. Legal rules must not be hard-coded in application source.
