# South Africa Launch – Legal & Policy Requirements

Reference statutes:
- Protected Disclosures Act 26 of 2000
- Proposed Protected Disclosures Bill (2026 draft)
- Employment Equity Act
- Labour Relations Act
- Occupational Health & Safety + Harassment Code (2022)
- POPIA + Information Regulator guidance

## Intake & Notices
- Provide PDA-compliant whistleblower notice (language, eligibility, confidentiality assurances).
- Include harassment Code obligations for confidentiality/sensitivity.

## Process Requirements
- Acknowledge reports within statutory timeframe (e.g., 7 days) – confirm with counsel.
- Provide feedback/outcome within 3 months unless legally constrained (align with EU directive when applicable).
- Offer clear routing for disclosures involving senior leadership (independent escalation).
- Support lawful external reporting guidance (regulators, ombuds, unions).

## Privacy & POPIA
- Operator/responsible-party roles defined; update operator agreement.
- Security compromise workflow (Regulator + data subjects “as soon as reasonably possible”).
- Data minimisation: store only necessary personal info; retention schedule.

## Evidence & Support
- Offer counselling/support info consistent with harassment Code, including remote-work and travel contexts (Teams/Slack, WhatsApp, business travel, conferences, customer sites).
- Preserve evidence per LRA/EEA and POPIA security safeguards; store immutable originals + working copies.
- Document retaliation-monitoring steps (check-ins, HR action correlation).

## Consultation
- Works council/union consultation where applicable; record meeting minutes.
- Document local languages to support and ensure notices accessible.

## Case Law References (Scenario Inputs)
- Kunene v Akani Egoli – occupational detriment after disclosure → retaliation tracking.
- Modika v IDC – systemic bullying allegations → conflict-aware routing.
- Pillay v Samancor Chrome – confidentiality charges post disclosure → policy reminders.
